## 🛠️ How to Contribute

1. **Find your assigned slot:** Check the **[Issues Tab](../../issues)** to find your assigned issue: `MUKTI Contribution #<ID> 🚀` to see your number and your assigned word.
2. **Fork this repository:** Click the **Fork** button at the top-right of this page.
3. **Clone your fork:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/Mukti-GitHub.git
   cd Mukti-GitHub
   ```
4. **Find your assigned line:** Open `README.md`, find your assigned number line under *The Secret Sentence* above (e.g. `3. 3`).
5. **Replace only your number:** Change your line to your assigned word (e.g. `3. brings`). Leave all other lines and numbers untouched!
6. **Commit and push:**
   ```bash
   git add README.md
   git commit -m "Add MUKTI contribution #<your-slot-number>"
   git push origin main
   ```
7. **Open a Pull Request:** Go to your fork on GitHub and click **Compare & pull request**.
8. **Automated verification:** The Mukti Bot will automatically validate your submission, merge your word, update the sentence, and comment with live progress! 🎉

---

## 📋 Contribution Guidelines

- Replace only your assigned number.
- Do not modify other participants' numbers or words.
- Do not change anything outside the contribution section.
- Submit your Pull Request to the original MUKTI repository.

**That's it! You've made your first contribution to MUKTI. 🎉**
